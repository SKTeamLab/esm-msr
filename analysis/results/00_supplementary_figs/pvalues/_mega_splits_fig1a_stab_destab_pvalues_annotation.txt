Statistical Annotation: _mega_splits_fig1a_stab_destab_pvalues.csv
=====================================================================
Figure / Panel: Supplementary Figure (_mega_splits_fig1), Panel a (Full MegaScale `mega_splits` test set: overall, stabilizing ddG > 0, and destabilizing ddG < 0 variants).

Metric Tested:
  Mean per-domain Spearman's rho across the 28 `mega_splits` test domains (with mean per-domain Pearson's r and RMSE in kcal/mol also reported).

Statistical Test & Resampling Procedure:
  Paired nonparametric domain-cluster x seed bootstrap (B = 10,000, shared Multinomial domain weights across all models; 3-seed retrained transfer models resampled jointly over training seeds).
  One-sided Phipson & Smyth (2010) p-value: p = (1 + #{b : delta^(b) <= 0}) / (B + 1).
  Bonferroni correction adjusts within each subset over all m = 8 comparator models present.
  `all_clean_IUT` gives Berger's (1982) Intersection-Union Test (p_IUT = max_j p_j) for beating all comparators simultaneously.
