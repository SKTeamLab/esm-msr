Figure 3, Panel d — ProteinGym multi-property Pareto frontier (Biophysical vs Functional, 176 unseen assays)
============================================================================================================

Biophysical macro-average Spearman's rho (Stability [n = 37] and Expression [n = 18]) versus functional macro-average Spearman's rho (Activity [n = 38], Binding [n = 13], and Organismal fitness [n = 70]) across all 105 evaluated models on the 176 unseen ProteinGym assays (41 of 217 assays excluded under the four-part sequence + structure homology filter; no large-protein drop). Public ProteinGym zero-shot models (n = 95) are colored by inference-time modality: Sequence-only (46; top method annotated: VespaG), Structure + Sequence without MSA (26; top method annotated: ProSST-2048), and MSA / Retrieval (23; VenusREM and S3F_MSA annotated). All 7 stability baselines with full ProteinGym coverage are explicitly labeled directly on the plot using their canonical manuscript colors and markers (and omitted from the legend): SPURS (176/176), MutateEverything (174/176), ThermoMPNN (176/176), GeoDDG-3D (174/176), Rosetta (174/176), ESM3dG (173/176), and ESMtherm* (173/176); red open rings denote documented training overlap (ESM3dG, ESMtherm*). ESM-MSR variants are plotted in canonical manuscript greyscale connected by the dotted sigma trajectory (ESM3-small-open [sigma = 0] -> ESM-MSR sigma = 0.5 -> ESM-MSR sigma = 1.0), with 95% joint assay x triplicate-seed bootstrap whiskers (B = 10,000). Across all 105 models, only three are Pareto non-dominated (black staircase): ESM-MSR sigma = 1.0 (Pareto bootstrap probability 100.0%), ESM-MSR sigma = 0.5 (99.9%), and VenusREM (MSA-based).
