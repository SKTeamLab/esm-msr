Figure 3, Panel a — External thermostability benchmarks
=======================================================

Pooled Spearman's rho across 8 external thermostability datasets (Ssym, S461, K2369, S783, S2648, Q3421, S8754, and PTMUL), ordered from highest to lowest ESM-MSR performance (mean of seeds 1-3). Whiskers show 95% mutation-level bootstrap intervals (seeds resampled jointly with bootstrap draws for triplicate retrained models). Singles models score the single-mutant benchmarks (hollow markers: ESM-MSR WT-view, MutateEverything Stage 1, ThermoMPNN, SPURS single); doubles-trained models score PTMUL (filled markers: ESM-MSR Dual-view, MutateEverything Stage 2, SPURS-multi; ThermoMPNN additive; Rosetta Cartesian ddG scaled by 1/2.94 throughout). SPURS uses the retrained model with trained adapter. Red open rings mark documented training overlap (GeoDDG-3D, ESM3dG; leak audit), with text indicating the most severe overlap tier and affected share (M = exact mutations, m = constituent singles, P = same protein, H = close homolog).
