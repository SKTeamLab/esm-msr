Figure 4, Panel a — Per-property rectified stability gain at k = 96 (ASA x hydrophobicity)
=========================================================================================

Rectified stability gain at top k = 96 single-mutant picks per Homology-Restricted MegaScale test domain (33 domains with structural/hydrophobicity features; 35,295 single mutants), stratified into four quadrants by hydrophobicity change (delta-hydrophobicity <= 0 vs > 0) and relative solvent-accessible surface area (relative ASA <= 0.5 vs > 0.5). Rectified gain is defined as sum max(ddG, 0) over every pick regardless of predicted sign. Boxes show the interquartile range with darker colored median lines; dots show individual domains; black horizontal bars with vertical 95% bootstrap CI whiskers (B = 1,000) and numerical labels show means over domains; dashed horizontal lines mark oracle means. The bottom strip ('Picks (%)') shows the percentage of each model's top-96 picks falling into each biophysical quadrant.
