Figure 3, Panel b — Cross-benchmark generalization (ProteinGym, Domainome, MGnify, Rocklin Designs)
====================================================================================================

Spearman's rho across held-out ProteinGym average (173 held-out assays), held-out Domainome average (436 held-out domains), and pooled inferred-ddG benchmarks (MGnify and Rocklin Designs). Held-out excludes every target homologous to the Homology-Restricted MegaScale training libraries under the four-part sequence + structure homology filter (any of: MMseqs2 E-value <= 1e-3, BLOSUM62 identity > 0.40 over the shorter sequence, Foldseek E-value <= 1e-3, or Foldseek TM-score > 0.70; excluding 40 homologous ProteinGym assays, 86 Domainome domains, and homologous MGnify/Rocklin scaffolds). Seeds 1-3 are averaged for retrained models. Singles versions score singles assays and Domainome (hollow markers on Domainome for two-version models); doubles/multi-mutant versions score ProteinGym multi-mutant assays and MGnify/Rocklin sets (ESM-MSR Dual-view combined head, SPURS-multi, ThermoMPNN-D, MutateEverything Stage 2, ProSST Dual-view on ProteinGym multi-mutant assays and Rocklin Designs; ProteinMPNN direct; Rosetta covers ProteinGym singles only, not Domainome). ESM-MSR sigma = 1, sigma = 0.5, and ESM3-small-open (sigma = 0, adapters off) use deployed readouts (combined on multi-mutant assays, WT head on singles). On MGnify and Rocklin Designs, one pooled Spearman rho is computed across all measured variants (whiskers resample scaffolds x seeds), reading ESM-MSR on the observed Tobit-linked scale h(dG_wt + latent) - dG_wt and other models raw. Red open rings indicate training overlap (ESM3dG was fine-tuned on MGnify stability data; 'train src').
