Figure 2, Panel e — Five-level pooled epistasis residual hierarchy benchmarks
=============================================================================

Pooled Spearman's rho across all five epistasis residual hierarchy levels on the Homology-Restricted MegaScale test set (13 domains; B = 1,000 domain x seed bootstrap; 3 training seeds averaged for retrained models). Levels are evaluated in pooled form: naive / raw rho, saturation-subtracted rho, pair rho across position-pair matrices with >= 20 doubles, line rho across pair-centred row/column lines with >= 3 cells, and cell rho pooled over all double-centred residual cells. Black overlay bars evaluate the unsaturated subset (dG_add > 0); the red dotted line marks the -dG_add floor ceiling. Includes the ESM-MSR context control (ESM-MSR ctx ctrl).
