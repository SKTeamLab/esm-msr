Figure 4, Panel c — Held-out Domainome single-mutant screening curves
======================================================================

Single-mutant screening efficiency on held-out Domainome proteins (436 proteins; single-mutant models only), evaluated across screening budgets K from 1 to 100 variants per protein. Top: hit rate (fraction of top-K picks with measured abundance greater than wild type). Bottom: P(at least one hit in top K). Shaded grey band indicates the 95% bootstrap confidence interval (B = 1,000) for ESM-MSR WT-view only; dotted black and dashed red lines indicate oracle and random selection.
