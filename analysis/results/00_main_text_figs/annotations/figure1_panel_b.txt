Figure 1, Panel b — Homology-Restricted MegaScale test set: stabilizing vs destabilizing variants
=================================================================================================

Mean per-domain Spearman's rho across the 33 Homology-Restricted MegaScale test domains (n = 48,740 total variants), split into stabilizing (ddG > 0; n = 7,417, 33 domains; hatched bars) and destabilizing (ddG < 0; n = 41,323, 33 domains; solid bars) subsets. Horizontal black ticks denote overall per-domain mean Spearman's rho across all test variants (n = 48,740). Models are grouped into Zero-shot (ProSST, ProteinMPNN, ESM3-small-open), Retrained on Homology-Restricted MegaScale (ESM-MSR, MutateEverything, ThermoMPNN, SPURS), and Supervised, not trained on MegaScale (GeoDDG-3D, Rosetta). Retrained transfer models average 3 training seeds; error bars show 95% paired domain x seed bootstrap confidence intervals. Singles models (WT-view / Stage 1 / single) score single mutants and doubles-trained models (Dual-view / Stage 2 / -D / -multi) score double mutants.
